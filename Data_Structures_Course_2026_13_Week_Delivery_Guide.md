# Delivery guide moved

The current course has **14 instructional weeks**. Use the
[14-week delivery guide](Data_Structures_Course_2026_14_Week_Delivery_Guide.md).

Week 12 covers binary search and ordinary BSTs. Week 13 covers left-leaning red–black (LLRB) tree search, insertion, and course review.

Prim, Kruskal, and Union-Find are Module 14. Earlier modules
retain their current order, including Module 7's linked queue example and the transfer of its FIFO rule
to Module 8's array queues, tree levels, and maze BFS.
