# Data Structures Course 2026 — Weekly Vocabulary and Anticipated Student Question Bank

## Purpose

No finite document can enumerate every sentence a student might form. This
bank instead aims to cover every materially distinct question intent that can
reasonably be anticipated in the 14-week course: conceptual, procedural,
technical, debugging, assessment, accessibility, security, and transfer
questions. Near-identical paraphrases are intentionally consolidated. The
current bank contains **848 distinct question prompts**.

Each teaching week now has its own page containing the vocabulary students
are expected to learn and that week's anticipated questions. Across the 14
pages there are **610 required vocabulary placements**, **41 optional
extension placements**, and **601 week-specific questions**. A term may recur
in a later week when retrieval or a more precise meaning is instructionally
important, so vocabulary placements are not a claim of 651 globally unique
words.

The bank contains questions, not canned answers. It is an instructor planning
resource, a source for FAQ development and retrieval prompts, and a way to
notice where course directions may need clarification. It must never be used
to imply that students may ask only listed questions or must consult a list
before requesting help.

## Suggested instructor use

Before each week, scan the relevant section and decide which questions should
be answered proactively, which should remain inquiry prompts, and which need a
just-in-time hint. After class, add genuinely new question intents rather than
every new wording. Repeated questions are evidence about instructional design,
not student deficiency.

Question clusters can also be used to prepare accessible FAQ pages, office-hour
prompts, misconception checks, autopsy debriefs, and anonymous question forms.
Any published answers should preserve the course's staged inquiry gates rather
than revealing later models before students have recorded their initial work.
