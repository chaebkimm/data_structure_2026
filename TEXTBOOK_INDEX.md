# Korean Data Structures Textbook

Chapter numbers identify resources; the teaching sequence follows the 13-week delivery guide. Module 14 is split between linked nodes in Week 7 and DSU with Kruskal in Week 13. For new concepts, we first show why they are needed, and then give them a name.

Before starting the course, first read about what data structures and efficiency mean in [How to Store Data Well](module_00_datastructure/textbook.md).
